# Measurement explanations

Educational reference bands, not diagnoses, aesthetic targets or exercise prescriptions.
No external links are rendered in the client UI, as requested. Sources retained here for maintenance (reviewed 2026-09-14):

- Body fat: Gallagher et al., AJCN 2000;72:694–701, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10966886/ . Conventional adult reference bands for ages 20–39, 40–59 and 60–79: men [8,20), [11,22), [13,25); women [21,33), [23,34), [24,36). These are provisional population references, not universally accepted personal targets. Measurement method and population affect interpretation.
- Context and manufacturer chart: https://tanita.eu/understanding-your-measurements/body-fat-percentage . Manufacturer diagrams may round thresholds differently; do not silently mix tables.
- Water: https://tanita.eu/understanding-your-measurements/body-water ; general adult male 50–65%, female 45–60%. Not age-stratified and not a diagnostic hydration test.
- BMI: https://www.nhs.uk/health-assessment-tools/calculate-your-body-mass-index/calculate-bmi-for-adults ; thresholds 18.5, 25, 30. No automatic risk assessment or ethnicity-specific adjustments.
- Muscle: https://support.tanita.eu/support/solutions/articles/60001496005-why-is-my-result-for-total-muscle-mass-so-high- ; device definitions and included water vary. No universal kg threshold.

The age used is at the last displayed measurement, not today. No guessed age/sex; missing information, minors, known pregnancy or out-of-range age do not receive a personalized fat band. Source references cannot cover all clinical circumstances; educational limitations remain visible. Other metrics display neutral first/last/change values in the chart's selected units, not a fabricated healthy/unhealthy score. Existing stored measurements are unchanged.
